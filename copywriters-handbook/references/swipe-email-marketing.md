# Swipe File: Email Marketing

## Full Email Example
**From:** Richard Stanton's Stock Speculator
**Subject:** 6 shocking financial forecasts

Dear [NAME],

What's in store for the U.S. economy?

A new special report from stock analyst Richard Stanton warns of 6 new economic threats to unfold within the next 6 to 12 months. Click here for a free copy: [URL]

"I personally expect this to be the most volatile market of any sort I have ever seen," says Richard, "and with a looming recession and fears of climate change, some of the biggest returns are now being made in solar, wind, and other renewable energy stocks."

Among Richard's observations and predictions...

*Pattern: FROM line identifies authority. Subject line uses number + curiosity. Lead delivers benefit immediately with a clickable link. First paragraph is a mini-version of the full pitch. Quote adds credibility and specifics.*

## Subject Line Examples
- "6 shocking financial forecasts" (number + curiosity + news)
- "Come on back to Idea Forum!" (re-engagement, casual, personal)
- "Adobe PageMill 3.0 limited-time offer!" (product + urgency)

## 4 U's Subject Line Scoring
**Weak:** "Free Special Report" → Urgent: 1, Unique: 2, Ultra-specific: 2, Useful: 2
**Strong:** "Free report reveals how to cut training costs 90% with e-learning" → 3+ on three U's

## FROM Line Options
| FROM | When to use |
|---|---|
| List Owner | List owner requires it; community of interest; popular quality site |
| Your Company | Well-known brand; market leader; recipients may think they're your customers |
| Your Name | Personal message; company not well-known to recipients |
| Team (e.g., "The Adobe Team") | Collaborative effort; product team credibility |

## Opt-Out Statement Example
"We respect your online time and privacy, and pledge not to abuse this medium. If you prefer not to receive further e-mails from us of this type, please reply to this e-mail and type 'Remove' in the subject line."

## Whitelist Request Examples
"To ensure our e-mail is delivered to your inbox, please add the e-mail address messages@ourcompany.com to your Address Book or junk filter settings."

## Email Structure Pattern
1. FROM + SUBJECT (the "envelope" — must compel opens)
2. First paragraph: mini-version of complete message + immediate response link
3. Expanded body: features, benefits, proof, details
4. Close: restate offer + response link (same as #2 repeated)
5. P.S.: urgency, bonus, or strongest benefit restated
6. Opt-out statement

## Key Rules
- 55-60 characters per line max (set margins at 20 and 80)
- Lead with killer benefit in first sentence
- Limit to 3 click-through links (majority of clicks come from first two)
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences for email)
- Tone: helpful, friendly, informative — not hard-sell
- Don't claim "the best" or "quality" — be specific about HOW
- FREE still lifts response despite spam filter risks (test it)
- Easy on ALL CAPS (reads as shouting; can trigger spam filters)
