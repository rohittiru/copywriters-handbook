# Swipe File: Brochures, Catalogs, and PDF Sales Materials

## Cover Headline Examples
**Strong:** "Now... you can enroll in this AARP Plan of Group Hospital Insurance — designed to help pay expenses your other insurance does not cover!" (Prudential) — Clear, specific benefit.

**Weak (gimmick):** "Forget about marriage... why not just 'get engaged'" — Opens to reveal a car rental pitch. Nothing to do with marriage. Reader feels misled.

**Worst (no headline at all):** Just the product name and company logo. This is like running an ad without a headline — wastes a valuable selling opportunity.

## Visual Cover Examples
- Montauk beach hotel: Beautiful color photo of waves on the shore. No words needed — the image IS the benefit.
- Alfalfa seeds: Two side-by-side field photos. Left: scrawny, weed-infested. Right: lush, healthy (planted with their seeds, 40% yield increase). Caption tells the story.

## Brochure Structure Example
A book reselling service for corporate librarians organized their brochure:
1. Summary of the service and why librarians need it
2. Six major benefits of using the service (numbered)
3. Details and logistics of how to use it
4. Pricing and ordering information

*Pattern: What is it → Why you need it → How it works → How to get it.*

## Key Rules from the Book
- "The letter sells, the brochure tells" — in a direct-mail package, the brochure supports the letter with details, specs, and visuals
- Start body copy on the front cover to hook readers before they open
- Put a caption on every photo — captions are read 2x more than body copy
- Avoid detailed technical calculations in selling pieces (put them in a separate technical document)
- Break copy into short sections with subheads — readers who only scan still get the gist
- Consider how sections will appear on physical pages — important info shouldn't be buried in a fold

## Catalog Copy Pattern
Each product listing is a mini-ad:
- Product name/headline with benefit
- Brief description (2-3 sentences)
- Key feature/benefit bullets
- Price and ordering info
- Cross-sell: "Works well with..." or "Customers also bought..."
