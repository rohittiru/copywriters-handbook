# Swipe File: Online Ads

## Banner Ad Best Practices
- Medium rectangle (300x250) has highest click-through rates
- Wide skyscraper (160x600) has second-highest
- Keep file size below 25KB
- Ads near the scroll bar can generate high click-through (pointer is already nearby)
- "Above the fold" ads get more views but fewer clicks; lower ads are more targeted
- Larger ads are more memorable but smaller ads can work with good content and positioning

## Banner Copy Techniques
- Text must be minimal with large impact
- Offer a benefit for clicking: "Get More Traffic"
- Ask a question (answer is on your site)
- Create urgency: "Limited Time Offer"
- Include a clear CTA button

## Key Insight: Interruption vs. Intent
Banner ads rely on interruption — the user is already on a page they want to be on. Search ads (Google PPC) capture intent — the user is actively searching. This is why:
- Banners work better for brand awareness (long-term, hard to measure)
- Search ads work better for direct response (immediate, measurable)
- Banners are most effective as retargeting (shown to previous visitors who already know you)

## Platform Ad Compliance
- Facebook rejects specific health/income claims → use indirect angles (food-based ads for weight loss instead of "lose 10 lbs")
- Google dropped 3.2 billion ads in one year for policy violations
- For each 10% closer to perfect compliance, response rates drop ~10%
- Smart marketers find "honest and clever ways" to get stronger, bolder ads approved

## The Click-to-Landing-Page Chain
Every online ad should lead to a dedicated landing page, not a homepage. The ad's message and the landing page's headline must match closely. This is called "message match" — breaking it kills conversion.
