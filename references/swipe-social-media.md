# Swipe File: Social Media

## Platform-Specific Post Examples (Same Topic, Different Angles)

**LinkedIn version:** "If you want to know more about how to create Google Ads, then check out our latest blog where we show you, step by step, how to create your ad. You will also learn about how to select the right keywords to use with your ad. Check it out! [link]"

**Facebook version:** "Check out our latest process for quickly creating Google Ads so you will get found! Click below on the link to find out more! [link]"

**Twitter version:** "Learn about our latest processes for designing Google Ads in record time. We also show you the best way to select your keywords to get you noticed in search results. [link]"

*Pattern: Same destination, different hooks. LinkedIn = detailed/educational. Facebook = casual/benefit-driven. Twitter = punchy/compressed.*

## Engagement-Driving Closers
- "What say you?"
- "What do you think?"
- "Do you agree or disagree?"
- "How does this sound to you — good, fair, bad, or terrible?"

## Five Rules for Facebook Conversations
1. Don't directly say or imply the other person is stupid for disagreeing
2. Don't dismiss a linked source as "false" without counter-evidence
3. You can't use logic to remove a belief not put there by logic — don't try
4. Don't state opinions as indisputable facts (Opinion: "filet mignon is better than chopped liver." Fact: "Bob Bly likes chopped liver better than filet mignon.")
5. Avoid controversial topics (politics, religion, race, gender) on business pages

## Content Calendar Pattern
- Post on regular schedule (e.g., Tuesday and Friday)
- Create themed series readers expect
- Each post links to blog or landing page (funnel approach)
- 80% value/education, 20% promotion

## Social Media Funnel Flow
Social post → Blog/Content → Email opt-in → Landing page → Sale

## Key Rules
- Business page, not personal page (unless your name IS the brand)
- Save long content for blogs — social readers want quick reads
- Be personable — write as if speaking to one individual
- Respond to comments and participate in conversations
- Avoid profanity and extreme language on business accounts
