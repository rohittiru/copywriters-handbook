# Swipe File: Landing Pages

## Landing Page Headline Examples
- "Become a Property Locator Today — and Make $100,000 a Year in the Greatest Real Estate Career That Only a Few Insiders Know About" (big promise + exclusivity + specific income)
- "FREE Ceramic Bearings Product Selection Guide" (free offer as hero, with product visual)
- "Now there is a better, easier, and more effective solution to wobbly restaurant tables that can irritate customers and ruin their dining experience: Table Shox, the world's smallest shock absorber" (problem-solution, ultra-specific)

## Credibility Builder Examples (first screen)
- Company logo + name (if well-known)
- University/institutional seal (upper left)
- 1-3 strong testimonials above or below the headline
- Pre-head summarizing credentials: "Trusted by 10,000+ companies since 2005"
- Media logos: "As seen in Forbes, Inc., TechCrunch"

## Bullet Format Example (Feature — Benefit)
"**Quick-release adhesive system** — your vehicle graphics stay clean and don't stick together"

Pattern: Bold feature first, then dash, then the customer benefit in plain language. Repeat for every key feature.

## Email Capture Copy Example (for non-buyers)
Floater window: "FREE REPORT: [Title of compelling report]. Enter your email below to get instant access."

*Floaters (slide onto screen from side/top) are NOT blocked by pop-up blockers, unlike pop-ups and pop-unders.*

## Problem-Solution Lead Example
"Now there is a better, easier, and more effective solution to wobbly restaurant tables that can irritate customers and ruin their dining experience: Table Shox, the world's smallest shock absorber."

Pattern: Name the specific problem → describe the pain → introduce the product as the fix. All in one or two sentences.

## Key Landing Page Rules
- NO navigation links — reader's only choice is respond or leave
- CTA buttons: big, bold, colorful; also hyperlink keywords in body copy
- Include toll-free number alongside digital CTA
- Money-back guarantee clearly stated (for sales pages)
- "No obligation" stated prominently (for lead-gen pages)
- Repeat CTA at beginning AND end of page
- Update copy periodically — outdated content kills credibility

## Conversion Benchmarks
- Businesses with 10-15 landing pages increase leads by 55%
- Those with 40+ landing pages get 12x as many leads as those with 5 or fewer
