# Swipe File: Video Scripts

## Video Sales Letter (VSL) Structure Example
For a financial newsletter:

1. **Hook (0-20 sec):** "Did you lose a fortune in the stock market crash? What if you could gain back everything you lost?"
2. **Problem agitation:** "While Wall Street insiders got richer, ordinary investors like you were left holding the bag..."
3. **Story/credibility:** "My name is [Name], and for the past 20 years I've helped over 10,000 investors..."
4. **Solution:** "I've developed a system that identifies the 3 signals that precede every major market shift..."
5. **Features/benefits:** Walk through each element of the product
6. **Proof:** "Last year, subscribers who followed my recommendations saw gains of..."
7. **Offer:** "Today you can get a full year's subscription for just $99..."
8. **Guarantee:** "If you're not completely satisfied within 90 days, I'll refund every penny."
9. **CTA:** "Click the button below to get started right now."
10. **Urgency:** "But I can only keep this offer open for the next 48 hours..."

## Key Production Insight
"Simple production often outperforms expensive production." Authenticity beats polish. A company owner speaking directly to camera with genuine enthusiasm (like the My Pillow commercials) can outsell a Hollywood-quality production.

## Four Learning Modes
People learn by: watching (video), listening (audio/podcasts), reading (text), and doing (experiential). Produce content in multiple formats to reach all audiences.

## Short-Form Video Pattern
- Open with most compelling visual or statement
- First 3 seconds must hook (social media auto-play is silent)
- Add captions/subtitles (most social video watched without sound)
- One message per video
- End with clear CTA
- Under 60 seconds for social; under 15 seconds for pre-roll ads
